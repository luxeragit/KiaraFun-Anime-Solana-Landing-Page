## THE MOST IMPORTANT CHANGE — CONTINUOUS SCROLL TYPOGRAPHY

The old implementation in `src/components/story.tsx` is not acceptable.

It uses three numbered scenes, each containing a separate statement, followed by transitions between them.

That is not the desired experience.

We want a **single continuous scroll-driven typography experience** in which the text and the surrounding product visualization evolve together.

### Desired interaction

As the user scrolls:

A large sentence is already present in the viewport.

The words begin in a low-emphasis state.

Individual words or short phrases become fully emphasized in sequence, based directly on scroll progress.

Previously revealed words remain readable as new words become active.

The visual emphasis progresses naturally through a continuous block of typography.

The page must not behave like a PowerPoint presentation.

Do not switch between three unrelated full-screen text slides.

Do not attach numbered chapter labels.

Do not make text simply fade in all at once when it enters the viewport.

The reveal must be genuinely scroll-scrubbed.

### Product-focused copy

Use this continuous typography:

"Create your token.
Launch on Solana.
Give your community something to build around."

This should feel like a single flowing editorial statement rather than three distinct slides.

Adjust line breaks intentionally across viewport sizes.

### Visual behavior

Pair the progressive text with a carefully designed future token-launch interface.

As the words become active, the conceptual product visualization may evolve:

During the token-creation phrase, emphasize the token identity area.

During the Solana-launch phrase, transition toward the launch configuration details.

During the community phrase, reveal the token's future profile and creator-update area.

Keep these interactions subtle and informative.

Do not use fabricated blockchain records or mock trading data.

The product visualization must remain visually coherent throughout the sequence.

### Technical implementation

Use GSAP ScrollTrigger.

Drive emphasis through scroll progress using `scrub`.

Apply ScrollTrigger to a continuous sequence, not separate numbered slide transitions.

Use per-word or per-phrase opacity and color interpolation, combined with subtle vertical movement only where appropriate.

A word should progress from low emphasis to full emphasis as its allocated scroll interval is reached.

Use native-flow text semantics and preserve accessible reading order.

If a sticky visual is necessary, keep pinning short, controlled, and responsive.

The user must not feel trapped in a full-screen scroll scene.

Do not create huge artificial blank spaces.

Do not add large `+=330%` scroll distances merely to prolong the effect.

The scroll should feel natural when moving forward, backward, or rapidly jumping through the page.

On mobile, adapt the sequence to an efficient natural-flow progressive reveal rather than forcing the desktop pinning behavior.

Provide a complete readable fallback for reduced motion.

This section is the signature interaction of the website. Spend meaningful engineering and design effort on it.
